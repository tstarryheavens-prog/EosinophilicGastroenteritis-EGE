## 1. Background: The Urgent Search for Non-Vasoconstrictive Abortives in Cluster Headache

Cluster headache (CH) is widely recognized as one of the most agonizing pain syndromes in clinical neurology, characterized by unilateral, excruciating periorbital and temporal piercing pain accompanied by ipsilateral cranial autonomic symptoms (lacrimation, conjunctival injection, nasal congestion).

While high-flow oxygen (12–15 L/min via non-rebreather mask) and subcutaneous sumatriptan (Imigran 3mg/6mg) represent gold-standard acute interventions, severe clinical challenges arise:

1. **Cardiovascular Restrictions**: Potent vasoconstriction mediated by 5-HT1B/1D receptors poses significant risk in patients experiencing concurrent morning hypertension surges or ischemic vulnerabilities.
2. **Medication Overuse Risk (Triptan Rebound)**: Frequent daily dosing (3–5 attacks per 24 hours during peak cluster bouts) rapidly precipitates medication-overuse chronification.
3. **Subcutaneous Injection Tolerance**: Repeated daily autoinjections cause localized tissue hardening and injection-site fatigue.

Consequently, modern neuropharmacological focus has shifted toward **non-vasoconstrictive mechanisms**: oral CGRP receptor antagonists (Gepants) and selective 5-HT1F receptor agonists (Ditans).

## 3. Real-World Attack Resolution Kinetics: Empirical Observations

Over multiple episodic cluster clusters, serial acute doses of **Nurtec 75mg ODT** and **Rayvow 100mg** were administered immediately at the prodromal phase (visual aura, nasal tingling, pain grade 2/10):

### A. Rayvow (Lasmiditan 100mg): Rapid Neural Blunting with CNS Dizziness
- **Onset of Pain Relief**: 25 to 35 minutes post-ingestion.
- **Pain Score Reduction**: Dropped severe peak pain (estimated 8–9/10) down to a tolerable dull pressure (2–3/10) within 60 minutes.
- **CNS Profile**: Marked dizziness, floating sensation (ataxia-like perception), and heavy somnolence within 45 minutes. Driving or technical computer work becomes impossible for 6 to 8 hours post-dose.
- **Best Use Case**: Nocturnal cluster attacks occurring between 01:00 and 04:00, where drug-induced somnolence facilitates uninterrupted rest without vascular concerns.

### B. Nurtec ODT (Rimegepant 75mg): Clean Hemodynamic Tolerability & Sustained Shield
- **Onset of Pain Relief**: 40 to 50 minutes post-dissolution on the tongue. Sublingual absorption without water avoids aggravating nausea during attack onset.
- **Pain Score Reduction**: Steady de-escalation of trigeminal throbbing. Complete pain freedom reached at 90 minutes.
- **CNS Profile**: Remarkably benign. **Zero dizziness, zero sedation, zero nausea, and zero blood pressure elevation.**
- **Long-tail Protective Effect**: Because of its ~11-hour half-life, a single morning dose of Nurtec consistently blocked secondary midday cluster breakthrough attacks (which normally occur 4 hours after sumatriptan clearance).

## 4. Blood Pressure Hemodynamics During Acute Dosing

Given the severe morning hypertension surges documented in our 12-year longitudinal vital dataset (Sys > 170 mmHg), monitoring systemic arterial pressure following abortive administration was critical:

- **Sumatriptan**: Acute administration elevated systolic pressure by an additional **+14 to +22 mmHg**, frequently pushing systolic readings into hypertensive crisis territory (185–195 mmHg).
- **Rayvow**: Neutral blood pressure profile (+0 to +3 mmHg deviation).
- **Nurtec**: Completely neutral hemodynamic response. In fact, by eliminating acute trigeminal pain arousal, systolic pressure settled downwards by **-18 mmHg** at 2 hours post-dose.

## 5. Strategic Patient Protocol: The Hybrid Escalation Approach

Based on 5+ months of clinical application in refractory cluster periods, the optimal non-vasoconstrictive management protocol consists of:

1. **Morning / Daytime Onset (Active Working Hours)**:
   - Immediate ingestion of **Nurtec 75mg ODT**.
   - Zero cognitive impairment allows continuation of daily activities.
   - 11-hour receptor blockade safeguards against midday attack clusters.
2. **Nocturnal Attack Onset (Severe Awakening Pain)**:
   - Rapid-action **Rayvow 100mg** combined with 15 L/min pure oxygen inhalation for 15 minutes.
   - Harnesses the somnolence profile to return immediately to sleep.
3. **Emergency Rescue (< 10 minutes required)**:
   - Reserve subcutaneous sumatriptan strictly for breakthrough attacks reaching 9/10 within minutes, keeping total weekly injections under 4 to avoid vascular remodelling.

In contrast, Nurtec primarily targets peripheral and perivascular CGRP receptors, yielding minimal central sedative effects.
